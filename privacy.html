Job Digest privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Job Digest is a personal tool used by its owner and the few people the owner sets it up for, each with their own Google account. This policy covers the Google user data it touches.

What it accesses

One Google permission, gmail.send.self, which lets it send email from a user's Gmail account to that user's own address and nowhere else. It doesn't ask for permission to read, search, change, or delete mail, contacts, or any other Google data.

How it uses that access

It sends each user one email a day, from their account to their own address. The tool writes that email itself; nothing in it comes from the mailbox.

Storage and sharing

It doesn't store, sell, or share any Google user data. Each user's Google sign-in token is kept privately in the owner's configuration, and each user can revoke theirs at any time from their Google Account, under third-party connections.

Who can use it

Its owner and the people the owner sets it up for. Job Digest isn't offered to the public.
